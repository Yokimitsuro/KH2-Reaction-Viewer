KH2 format readers

The original-asset archive reader follows khdays-port's
installer/collection_archive.py. The archive table comes from the player's
installed game executable. The viewer's BDX reader follows the interpreter
layout in tools/mods/kh2_bdx.py. Call shapes were extracted from the Steam
executable with tools/research/kh2_ai.py --exe-calls. The international font
groups are from port/ui/reaction_command.cpp. These references are from
khdays-port revision 630768fb9d1fb689474ddb9ee00cb5df7d8a1d91.

Object name references: OpenKH KH2 object dictionary.
https://openkh.dev/kh2/dictionary/obj.html
See Licenses/OPENKH_LICENSE.txt and Licenses/OPENKH_NOTICE.txt.

Source revisions

The vendored khdays-port model and motion readers and pose helpers are based
on commit e5a8b7394f4e318c8a9ea64339c4a928368d3396. The viewer extends the
MDLX reader with package textures and native skin weights, and the motion
reader with the sampled matrices found in the game's MSET files.
The PAX effect interpreter is from the same revision.
https://github.com/Yokimitsuro/khdays-port/tree/e5a8b7394f4e318c8a9ea64339c4a928368d3396
See third_party/khdays-port/LICENSE.

The vendored stb_image.h is from stb commit 2c980bb59875b0d32144a71867fbdebb2f77cd20.
https://github.com/nothings/stb/tree/2c980bb59875b0d32144a71867fbdebb2f77cd20
See third_party/stb/LICENSE.
